Two cautions before you compare yourself to this table. First, these are posted ranges, not offers — companies that publish ranges tend to be the ones with pay-transparency practices, and the posting is a band the hiring manager expects to negotiate inside. Second, the family labels are ours: a "solutions engineer" lands in engineering here, a "technical account manager" in sales. Read the row that matches your actual work, not your job title.

Within engineering the ladder is steep and compressed: the p25 to p75 span is $185,000 to $277,500 — a quarter of engineering postings sit above the median for the whole dataset.
