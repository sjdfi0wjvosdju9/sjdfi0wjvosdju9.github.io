Where the numbers come from

We read salary ranges that companies publish in their own job postings (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workable), normalise them to annual USD, and take the midpoint of each posted range. If a posting has no salary, it is not in the dataset. Sample size is printed on every table because a median over 14 postings is not a median over 629.
