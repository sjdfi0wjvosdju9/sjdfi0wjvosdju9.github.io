What we deliberately do not do

  • We never submit bids, applications, or bug-bounty reports. The tools read public listings; you do the sending.
  • We never guess a salary that a posting does not state, and we drop implausible ones rather than publish them.
  • We do not promise a posting is still open by the time you act on it.
  • No browser automation, no proxies, no captcha solving. Every source is a public, keyless endpoint.

Notes on how it breaks

  • Job boards rotate their slugs. When a company vanishes we report it in failedCompanies instead of silently returning less.
  • Workday needs the full careers URL — its board-host prefix cannot be guessed reliably; 192 guesses produced exactly one hit.
  • Bug-bounty programmes publish a reward range. For ranking we use the geometric mean of the range, not the ceiling.
