Questions people actually ask

What does asana publish as an annual salary?

Across 70 postings that state a pay range, the median midpoint is $194,500. The middle 50% sits between $155,000 and $225,500, and the 90th percentile is $324,150. All figures are annualised and converted to USD.

How many asana postings actually publish pay?

70 of the postings we read from greenhouse carried a range on the day we measured (2026-10-24). The rest did not publish pay at all, so they are not in these numbers.

Which roles at asana publish the highest salaries?

engineering is the largest family in this sample (23 postings, median $194,500). The table above lists every family we saw and the highest individual ranges.

Is this what asana actually pays?

No. This is what the company itself published in its job postings, normalised to a yearly USD range. Real offers vary by level, location and equity, and many postings omit pay entirely.

Get the data

These 70 rows are part of one CSV covering every company we read: the full dataset (salary-ranges-full.csv) under CC BY 4.0. Or pull it into Python:

pip install https://sjdfi0wjvosdju9.github.io/packages/salaryranges-0.1.1-py3-none-any.whl
import salaryranges
salaryranges.by_company("asana")

If you want the same numbers refreshed on a schedule, including the postings that never publish pay, that is the paid part: ATS Job Feed ($0.0015 per returned job).

Read from the company's own public job feed on 2026-10-24. Companies change their postings and their pay bands, so treat this as a snapshot, not a contract. Only postings that state a range are counted — a low count here often means the company publishes pay rarely, not that it pays little.
